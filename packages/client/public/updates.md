# Realm News & Updates

### ⚔️ Pre-Alpha Patch 0.8.2 - Party Quests & Realm Tools
*August 5, 2026*

Greetings Adventurers! Welcome to **Shadows of Eldor** pre-alpha. We have deployed significant updates across questing, class tuning, and server infrastructure.

#### Key Highlights
- **Party Quest Proximity Sharing**: Quests accepted or progressed near party members within **40 meters** automatically share progress!
- **Class Spell Tuning**:
  - **Paladin**: *Holy Shield* damage mitigation boosted to **50%**.
  - **Cleric**: *Circle of Healing* range increased to **15m**.
  - **Ranger**: *Beast Mastery* wolf companion pathfinding and threat hold enhanced.
- **Wilderness Alchemy**: New recipes available for *Flask of the Titan* (+60 Max HP) and *Free Action Potion* (slow immunity & +15% speed).

### 🧪 Developer Insights: Alchemy & Gathering
*July 28, 2026*

Wilderness survival is essential in Eldor. Remember to harvest **Berry Bushes**, **Pine Trees**, and **Stone Outcrops** to stock up on crafting reagents.

> *Tip: Slaying undead skeletons yields Ancient Dust, a critical ingredient for brewing Runic Healing & Mana Potions!*
